Who we are

A creative studio with a business brain.

Aura Events & Consulting was founded in Lahore in 2024 with a simple observation: Pakistani brands host thousands of events every year, yet few are designed to move the business forward. We exist to close that gap — pairing the craft of event management with the discipline of business consulting.

Today, our eleven-person team delivers corporate summits, weddings, exhibitions and hybrid experiences across Pakistan, while our consulting desk helps startups and SMEs launch, price and grow with confidence. One team, two crafts, a single standard: every engagement should look stunning and pay for itself.
